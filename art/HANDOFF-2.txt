Delivered

units.png + units.json: complete 13-unit roster, 104 hand-authored native
16x16 frames. Original row/column order retained. All 13 IDs are in
availableUnits; unavailableRows is empty. Runtime copies in game/art/ match.

red hood / white phone; separate moustached head, bright belly and blue jeans;
all-pink cat, white muzzle, curled tail and dark bag; bright police helmet and
shield; blue truck, white stripe and water jet. The other eight follow the
same outline, palette and scale. All frames have an empty bottom pixel row.

blue CRS capture-flag variant (36 frames). Default capture_flag is Lycéens.

Seine x4, north and south quay overlays, vertical bridge. Road mask bits are
N=1, E=2, S=4, W=8. Quays are transparent overlays, not extra water tiles.

props.png + props.json: all 15 required buildings / shops, plus five unbranded
shop variants (sneakers, phone, brasserie, boulangerie, kebab). Cells are 16x24.
Draw at tile y minus 8px, in map-row order. The 4x6 top-right pennant gap is
empty in every frame. The existing pennant position must be moved into that
gap when integrating: gap coordinates are relative to the sprite, not tile.
