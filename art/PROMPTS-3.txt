BRIEF 3 — GENERATION SPECIFICATIONS
Mode: built-in image_gen. No API/CLI fallback.
All paintings: 1991 Amiga / LucasArts pixel-painted style, fixed palette from
art/palette.json, deliberate flat colour clusters, no dithering, no gore.
The original brief supplies the scene descriptions. Vex's Velvet Alcove was
visually inspected as the tonal/style reference. No press photos were used.

scenes-first-four.png
2 columns x 2 rows. Four backgrounds intended for 96x66 native output, subjects
in the upper half / edges, bottom eight rows plain asphalt. Row-major:
République (hi-vis Marianne, climbing youths, smoke and burning kiosk), Capucines
(Opéra dome, street fire, youth holding blank sign), Louvre (pyramid, barriers,
bins, tourists, blank FERMÉ sign), Vélib' (green rental bicycle pile, fire).
Signs left blank in generated artwork for accurate native pixel typesetting.

scenes-rest.png
2 columns x 5 rows. Row-major: Champs (Arc and barricade), Halles (broken sneaker
shop and kicking youth), Rivoli café (two calm coffee drinkers, morning after),
boulangerie (fire, baguettes), Île (Notre-Dame, CRS line, water cannon), guerre
civile (burning car, blank wall for graffiti), pont (stone balustrade, Seine,
green gas), jardin (Tuileries trees and burning bin), Beauvau (gates, tricolour,
vans), lycée (blue tarp, damaged ceiling, blank banner). Same clear ground and
upper-half composition rules. Generated row boundaries were cropped explicitly
in build-scenes3.cjs rather than assuming an even layout.

title-source.png

ivory/gold/fire-bevel lettering; THE GAME underneath. République at purple dusk,
Marianne wearing hi-vis, police left and youths right, firelight at edges.
Blank dark menu band at y150-226 framed with brass. No menu labels in source.

victory-lyceens-source.png
384:240. Night République celebration, youths climbing Marianne, hi-vis vest,
amber/pink/blue fireworks, warm lanterns. Blank dark brass-framed result band
at y150-200. No victory wording or logo.

victory-crs-source.png
384:240. Dawn Haussmann boulevard, municipal sweepers in green, small cleaning
vehicle, calm café couple among broken windows, distant departing CRS. Quiet
morning-after comedy. Blank dark result band at y150-200, brass frame, no text.

help-source.png
384:240. Flat cream Préfecture memo on walnut desk. NOTE DE SERVICE header,
tricolour detail, paperclip, edge wear, seal and coffee ring at bottom. Blank
body x14-370, y30-200 for sixteen lines. Native cleanup relocated decorations
below the body area and flattened the writing surface.

FINAL ASSEMBLY
Paintings reduced with Lanczos then nearest palette matching, without dither.
All UI chrome and font pixels authored natively. Previews enlarged by nearest
neighbour only. Production output and integration metadata live in art/.
